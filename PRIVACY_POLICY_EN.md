# Privacy Policy (Shopping Genius)

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app **Shopping Genius**.

## 1) Summary

- The app is primarily designed to work locally on your device.
- The app does not require user accounts, and it does not include ad or tracking SDKs.
- No personal data is actively collected by the developer and sent to own servers.
- Internet access is only used for specific features (see below).

## 2) What data does the app process?

The app mainly processes **content data** that you enter or import yourself:

- shopping lists
- grocery/product items
- categories
- local settings
- locally stored icons/images

This data is generally stored locally on your device.

## 3) Permissions

The app currently uses the following permission:

- `android.permission.INTERNET`

According to the current manifest, the app does not request particularly sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, or camera.

## 4) How is internet access used?

The app uses internet access for the following features:

1. **Updating app content**
   - Fetching product, category, and icon data from the project/release environment.
2. **Optional icon image search (DuckDuckGo)**
   - If you use image search, your search query is sent to DuckDuckGo.
3. **Voice input (Android system feature)**
   - If you use voice input, speech data is processed by the speech recognition service available on your device.

Note: Based on the current project state, the developer does **not** operate a dedicated telemetry/tracking server for usage analytics.

## 5) Data sharing with third parties

The developer does not actively share your shopping list content with third parties.

However, some optional features may involve third-party services, for example:

- search queries for optional DuckDuckGo image search
- voice input data handled by your Android speech recognition provider

In such cases, the privacy terms of the respective third-party providers also apply.

## 6) Ads, tracking, analytics

Based on the current project state, the app contains:

- no ad SDKs
- no user tracking/profiling
- no analytics or crash-tracking integrations for personal profiling

## 7) Backups and device transfer

Depending on device settings, Android version, and account configuration, Android system backup may include app data.

This means locally stored app data can be processed as part of Android backup/restore infrastructure when backup is enabled on the device.

## 8) Your control and deletion options

You can control and delete data at any time, for example by:

- deleting entries in the app
- clearing app data in Android settings
- uninstalling the app

## 9) Children

The app is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10) Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if features or legal requirements change.
